About Me

Hi, I'm Lent 👋
Nice to meet you.

A customer-focused Virtual Assistant, Executive Assistant, and Customer Support Professional — someone who genuinely loves helping people and businesses thrive.

My professional journey began in the BPO industry, where I built a strong foundation supporting U.S.-based clients in customer service and technical support roles at Sykes, Teleperformance, and ePerformax. That experience sharpened my communication, problem-solving, and adaptability — qualities I carry into every project today.

Now working fully remote as a Freelance Executive Assistant, I partner with businesses of all sizes to manage daily operations, generate leads, coordinate recruitment, build websites, execute email marketing campaigns, and create engaging content that drives real results.

What makes me different? It's the combination of sharp attention to detail, genuine care for my clients' success, and the kind of proactive communication that keeps everything running smoothly — even across time zones.

Whether you need a reliable assistant, a customer support specialist, or a creative collaborator, I'm committed to delivering quality work and making every interaction a positive one. Let's turn your goals into results.
